Sat dinner · 등촌샤브 칼국수 + 조개찜

Order: shabu (meat + veggies) → 칼국수 in the broth → 볶음밥 to finish. Keep the broth mild for the kids.

샤브 + 칼국수 K

  • 샤브용 소고기 (thin-sliced beef) 2 lb
  • 미나리 2 bunches
  • 버섯 (느타리, 팽이, 새송이) ~1.5 lb
  • 배추 ¼ head, 청경채 6, 숙주 1 bag, 대파 2
  • 칼국수 면 3 packs
  • 육수: 사골곰탕 2 packs (or a 등촌 샤브 meal kit if they have one)
  • 볶음밥: 김가루, 참기름 (+ rice, eggs)

조개찜 K

  • Live clams (바지락/littleneck) 3 lb, plus whatever you catch
  • 청양고추 3, 대파 1, 마늘 1 head
  • 청주 or 소주 ½ cup, butter

🍶 안주 (to share with drinks) K

  • 부추전: 부침가루, 부추 2 bunches, 양파 1, 청양고추 (optional: shrimp)
  • 계란말이: kids love it too (eggs A)
  • Drinks: 소주, 맥주, 막걸리 (goes with 부추전)

Make the 안주 while the broth heats, and eat them first with drinks. The shabu comes after.

🐚 Clams: soak in salted water (2 Tbsp salt per quart) for 1–2 hrs in a dark spot to purge sand. Saturday's catch only gets about an hour, so the store clams are the main batch. Keep live clams in the cooler, not in sealed bags.
🦪 Buy clams near the Airbnb (if you skip the Korean market or need more). Call ahead to check for live clams.
